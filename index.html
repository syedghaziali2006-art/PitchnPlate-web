SPORTS & RECREATION

Built for Real Sports Lovers

Designed for professional enthusiasts and weekend warriors alike. Quality artificial turf, full safety netting, and floodlights for midnight matches.

Indoor Cricket

Indoor Cricket Arena

Enclosed high-tension safety netting, premium green turf, and professional pitch markings. Play authentic tape-ball or match cricket with high-intensity non-glare floodlights.

  • ✓ Full-height box cricket netting
  • ✓ RSC bats, wickets, and match balls available
  • ✓ 24/7 Floodlit night sessions
Futsal Turf

Indoor Futsal Ground

Fast-paced mini football action on durable shock-absorbent turf. Rebound walls, sturdy goalposts, and ample space for 5v5 or 6v6 squad showdowns.

  • ✓ Smooth shock-absorbing turf
  • ✓ Ideal for competitive leagues & casual kickabouts
  • ✓ Night floodlighting with zero blind spots
Café & Screening

Match Screenings & Café

Catch ICC World Cup, PSL, and Champions League games on the giant outdoor projector screen. Enjoy hot tea, burgers, fries, and play board games on custom Ludo tables.

  • ✓ Giant open-air live sports projection
  • ✓ Fresh burgers, rolls, chai & snacks
  • ✓ Vibrant chill vibe with custom Ludo tables
Family Lounge

VIP Family Lounge

A dedicated, beautifully illuminated seating area featuring plush velvet sofas and fairy lights overlooking the pitches. A safe and comfortable space for families and guests.

  • ✓ Separate comfortable velvet sofa seating
  • ✓ Direct vantage point of ongoing games
  • ✓ Warm canopy lighting and family-friendly environment
